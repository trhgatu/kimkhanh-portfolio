# Kim Khanh — Personal Archive

A bespoke digital home created for Kim Khanh — a warm, botanical space for flowers, places, words, and small everyday joys.

The experience brings together editorial typography, tactile paper-like surfaces, an expressive 3D flower study, and gentle motion inspired by the rhythm of turning through a personal scrapbook.

## The experience

- A custom floral letterform and softly staggered hero introduction
- An interactive botanical composition with a pollen-like pointer trail
- Scroll-linked flowers and organic section transitions
- A pinned editorial layout that moves from personal introduction into archive
- Scrapbook-inspired collections built around memories and small observations
- A responsive, accessible experience with reduced-motion support
